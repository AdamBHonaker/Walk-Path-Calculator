# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**, as its code of
conduct. The canonical text lives at:

<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>

By participating in this project — opening issues, submitting pull requests,
commenting in discussions — you agree to abide by its terms.

## Reporting

Instances of behavior that violate the Contributor Covenant may be reported
to the project maintainer at **wayfarer.atlas@gmail.com**. All reports will
be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer is
obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any
incident.

## Enforcement

Enforcement follows the Contributor Covenant 2.1 community-impact guidelines
(correction → warning → temporary ban → permanent ban) linked from the
canonical text above. The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and
enforcing these standards and will take appropriate and fair corrective
action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful.
